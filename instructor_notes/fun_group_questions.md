## About

These questions are, in part, icebreakers. But instead of focusing on "get-to-know-you" (which they will do naturally on their own), these questions focus primarily on:

- Problem solving together
- Making concrete from the abstract
- Fun, engaging
- Course/topic relevant

## Fun Group Questions

- Derive an equation for how much wood a woodchuck could chuck if a woodchuck could chuck wood.
    - What conditions would need to change for a woodchuck to be able to chuck wood?
    - What qualities are most important for the woodchuck to have to increase their wood-chucking capacity?
    - How do you measure wood-chuck ability?

- How do you determine the winner in a 3-way rock paper scissors match?
    - Does it change if you add lizard and spock?
