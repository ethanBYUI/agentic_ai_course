

## Use Case Teaching Order

Industry problem
      ↓
Instructor/example demonstration
      ↓
Student runs a minimal example
      ↓
Student modifies or builds something
      ↓
Student applies the idea in assessment/project

## Practice, Feedback, Transfer

Quality of the three is not sufficiently shown. Perhaps this is a good model:

Competency
  ├── Introduced by activity/material
  ├── Practiced by activity
  ├── Assessed by assessment
  └── (sometimes) Transferred through project/use case
