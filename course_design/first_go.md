My initial thoughts (not requirements) on the difference between the first semester this course is offered and future courses. 

As the course hardens, the pacing becomes clear. Anticipating pacing is difficult.

Rather than having a strict daily schedule (this is taught this day), we build in wiggle room into each unit. Ethics days can be moved as we see fit. Ethics discussions are preceded by a prep reading and anteceded by work time. Work time can be an extra opportunity to teach.

If students are turning things in to Canvas, flexibility becomes difficult to maintain. Either only major projects are kept in Canvas, or if they are turning things in regularly, it can be unnamed - defined only by its date. It could then be added or deleted without shifting all future assignments.

In the future, clarity in Canvas reduces cognitive load considerably, and if the pacing is solid, flexibility is not as needed.
